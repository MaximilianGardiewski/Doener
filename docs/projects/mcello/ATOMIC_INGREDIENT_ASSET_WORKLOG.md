### Shared generation contract

Every prompt below is completed with the same three clauses. This is the blueprint's whole point: identical camera and light are what let independently generated assets stack without manual distortion.

```
<camera>     Isometric view, exactly 45 degree angle, horizon locked, no perspective drift
<lighting>   Studio lighting, direct warm top light, soft downward shadow, consistent falloff
<background> Solid black
```

Additional constraint carried by every subject: the object forms **one horizontal layer** that spans most of the frame width, sits almost flat, shows visible thickness at its front edge, is fully inside the frame, and touches nothing else.

Shared negative prompt, unchanged from the first pass and extended for the layer format: second copy of the subject, plate, bowl, board, tray, packaging, other foods, bread unless it is the subject, hands, people, text, logos, watermark, cast shadow on a surface, tilted or vertical camera, illustration, cartoon, CGI render look.

Everything that went wrong in this run, recorded rather than hidden:

- **The briefed prompts produced the wrong subject three times out of four.** Cucumber came back halved lengthwise instead of as round slices, lettuce came back as whole heads although the prompt said "never a whole head", and meat came back as a geometric stack of dried strips. All three prompts were sharpened and regenerated; the revised wording is stored per manifest as `briefPrompt` with `promptRevisedFromBrief: true`.
- **The döner meat prompt was refused by Firefly's usage policy.** The web interface answered "Ein oder mehrere Wörter entsprechen möglicherweise nicht den Nutzungsrichtlinien und wurden entfernt". Dropping "veal" and "kebab" in favour of "roasted rotisserie meat strips" passed.
- **The web interface returns 512 × 512, not the 2048 × 2048 the API produced for the first eight.** Toggling the fast-mode switch did not change it, and requesting a 2048 rendition returned the same 512 pixels, so the source really is 512. The owner accepted upscaling; every affected manifest records this under `sourceResolutionNote`. These four masters are measurably softer than the other eight.
- **Two generations were wasted.** The Firefly prompt field is a `textarea` inside a shadow DOM, so simulated typing never reached it and the generate button re-ran the previous prompt twice. The fix was setting the value through the native setter and dispatching `input`/`change`, and verifying the field content before every further generation.
- **Tone still is not uniform.** Measured mean subject luminance of the finished masters: cucumber 160 and garlic 171 sit above the 129–146 band, falafel 91 below it. Cucumber and garlic are genuinely pale ingredients and falafel is genuinely dark, so this was accepted rather than forced.
- **Camera axis is not uniform either.** The three regenerated vegetable and meat layers came back closer to a top-down view than the briefed isometric 45 degrees. Inside the stack between base and lid this reads acceptably, but it is a real deviation from `D076`'s single camera contract.

Deviations from the original brief, recorded rather than hidden:

- The asset id for the extra tomato row is `ingredient.tomato.layer.extra`, not `…layer-extra`. The governed id pattern `^ingredient\.[a-z0-9.]+$` allows no hyphen, and softening that guard was the worse option.
- Tight extraction and the 1024 × 1024 normalization ran locally and deterministically from the alpha bounding box instead of through an Adobe crop with visually estimated bounds. Both steps are pure geometry, they are recorded as `local-…` in every manifest, and the result is exact rather than eyeballed.
- The generation request IDs are not observable from this session — the generations ran in Codex. Each manifest carries the Firefly asset URN and marks `requestIdCaptured: false` with the reason, and stores the brief prompt as `briefPrompt` rather than claiming a verbatim prompt it cannot prove.
- The eight processed masters were not added to the family board. The raw generations are already in Adobe; the processing provenance lives in the manifests.
- Codex's raw output had an inconsistent light axis, which is exactly what the shared camera and lighting clause exists to prevent. Measured mean subject luminance before correction: onion 64, falafel 40, hot sauce 78, extra tomato 90, curry 106, against 129–146 in the existing governed masters. Tone was aligned per asset; onion and falafel additionally needed saturation back after brightening.
- Two subjects have real defects: the onion rings run past the left and right frame edge instead of sitting fully inside it, and the cutout clipped a hard edge on the right of the curry layer. Both are usable at layer scale but are candidates for regeneration.
