# Business Web CMS Builder
Treat the old site as a functional/content baseline, not a visual template. Research the real business; never invent facts. Put volatile data in CMS. Prefer structured content and constrained blocks. Enforce authorization in backend/database. Preserve useful URLs and workflows. Keep Git, migrations, env docs and tests as durable source of truth.

## Phases
0. Mission and success criteria.
1. Business + legacy research.
2. Visitor/moderator/admin roles.
3. CMS content model.
4. Information architecture + constrained page builder.
5. Design foundations/screens with Figma or equivalent.
6. Portable TypeScript/React/Postgres/Supabase-style implementation; prove one complete vertical slice first.
7. Accessibility, SEO, performance, privacy.
8. Protected forms/workflows + spam/rate strategy.
9. GitHub/Git workflow and documentation.
10. Visual refinement with optional design tooling.
11. Migration/launch only after security/content/rollback gates.

Always report completed, verified, blocked and next-safe-step separately.
