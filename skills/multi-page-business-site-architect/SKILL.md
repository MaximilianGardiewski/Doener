# Multi-Page Business Site Architect
Primary nav items should be real routes. Homepage is orientation, not duplication. Keep one visible H1 per public page. Put complete workflows/forms/calculators on destination pages. Preserve useful slugs. Keep desktop/mobile IA equivalent.

Workflow: audit routes/nav/home/CMS/metadata/redirects; define canonical routes; compress homepage; upgrade destination pages with route-specific hero/H1/content/CTA; update nav; validate internal links, metadata, sitemap, redirects, H1 counts and overflow. Do not modify auth/RLS merely for IA changes.
